# Historical Monarchs
A mod for the video game Victoria 3: 
[Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3757464105)

## Mod contents
This mod adds historical rulers, heirs, chains of succession and potentially, historical government types, custom character DNA, narrative events and historical regents, to many monarchies in the game. It also adds a number of game rules to add the ability to turn on and off some features.

### Game rules
- A game rule control whether or not the monarchs and governors of certain countries die in the year they historically did.
- Era names: in Victoria 3 it is inconsistent whether or not an East Asian nation's monarchs use their era name or personal name. The mod has three game ruler on a country-by-country basis for users to set their preferences for the following countries:
  - China (vanilla default: era name);
  - Korea (vanilla default: personal name);
  - Dai Nam (vanilla default: personal name).  
 Monarchs who use their era names have their last names removed, this could potentially cause empty spaces when an event invokes their last names.

### Decisions
The mod includes flavoured decisions for Great Britain and Belgium, which allows them to rename their branches of the House of Saxe-Coburg-Gotha in case of a war with Germany, like they historically did.

### Federal monarchies
Additionally –inspired by the daimyo system of vanilla Japan– this mod includes a historical magnate generation mechanic for German federal monarchies (North German Confederation, South German Confederation, German Empire, Holy Roman Empire, Confederation of the Rhine), so the historical monarchs of certain German states should exist as magnates and politicians with their appropriate title, clothing, and chains of succession (e.g. the kings of Saxony will exist as magnate type characters within Germany, if another German country forms it). Since only one magnate can own manor houses in a single state, only the largest German state's monarch is generated in that state region (e.g. only the grand dukes of Mecklenburg-Schwerin, not the grand dukes of Mecklenburg-Sterlitz).
Thus, provided they are not the ruler of the country, the following German federal monarchs are generated as magnates:
- King of Bavaria,
- King of Württemberg,
- King of Saxony,
- King of Prussia,
- Grand Duke of Baden,
- Grand Duke of Oldenburg,
- Grand Duke of Hesse
- Grand Duke of Mecklenburg-Schwerin,
- Duke of Anhalt,
- Duke of Brunswick,
- Prince of Lippe.
